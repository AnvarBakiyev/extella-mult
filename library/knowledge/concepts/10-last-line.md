# PUPPET: the last line
tags: last line, turn, punchline, ending
Decide it first; build the film backwards to it.
2-8 words, never over 10. Plain, concrete, said flat: start it with [deadpan] or [tired].
It sits in the last 3-6 s of the film, alone in its shot, after a 2-3 s silent shot.
It never explains the joke. Nobody reacts to it after; fade out 0.4 s after it ends.
It reveals a fact that reframes everything: who the guest really is, what the prize is, where the advice leads.
Good: «На молоток.» «Пробовал. Там ресторан.» «Не могу. Уйду — займут.» «Человек. Вышло дешевле.»
Bad: a moral, a question to the viewer, a pun, a long sentence, a line that repeats the setup.
Camera: snap at its start (snap=<line at>), or a still close-up if the scene was loud.
Music: already stopped.
Who says it: the format's turn speaker (search "PUPPET format" for "turn").
