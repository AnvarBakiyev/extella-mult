tags: format, F10, F11, F12, погода, игровое шоу, пресс-конференция
F10 Weather 42s: ch_kirill, pl_weather_studio. "Добрый вечер" 0-4 still | map 4-15 pan | advice 15-28 more and more serious, push | guest (ch_cloud, ch_marmot) 28-36 whip | turn 36-42 tomorrow's forecast scares in a new way, snap. Turn: forecaster or guest.
F11 Game show 45s: ch_inna, pl_game_show, music bright. Opening 0-5 pull | question 5-12 push | answers 12-28 contestants in turn, whip | super prize 28-36 snap | turn 36-45 prize or right answer, still, silence. Turn: host.
F12 Press conference 42s: ch_pavel, pl_press_room. Statement 0-8 still | question 8-16 hall handheld | smooth empty answer 16-24 push | awkward question 24-32 handheld push | turn 32-42 slip of the tongue or honest answer, then leaves, snap. Turn: press secretary.
