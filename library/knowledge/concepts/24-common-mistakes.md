# PUPPET: common mistakes to avoid
tags: mistakes, pitfalls, review, before check
Before you run check, scan the program for these:
- Lines too long: more than 2 words per second of shot. Split or cut words before check, not after.
- say on a place set (pl_...): places have no mouth. Use voice, or show the speaker's ch_ set.
- Shot times that do not touch, or a gap after a silent beat. Each shot starts where the last ended.
- at= written in shot seconds (at 0.3s in every shot). Use film seconds: shot start + 0.3.
- snap= outside its own shot. snap is a film second inside the shot.
- Music under the last line. Stop it 3-6 s earlier.
- The same camera on every shot. Vary: still host, pushing guest, one snap at the end.
- A last line that explains, moralises or asks the viewer. Cut it to the fact.
- A host who jokes. The host is straight; only the situation is absurd.
- Real names, brands, cities, countries, religion. Replace with invented ones.
- Film shorter than 30 s or longer than 60 s. Aim for 40-50 s.
- Prose around the program. Output only the program.
