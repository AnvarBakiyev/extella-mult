# PUPPET format F19-F21: family dinner, school lesson, doctor
tags: format, F19, F20, F21, семейный ужин, школьный урок, врач
F19 Family dinner 45s: pl_dining. Gathering 0-5 pan down | small talk 5-18 whip between lines | question 18-25 "ну, как у тебя дела?" push | confession 25-37 still, silence | turn 37-45 the eldest's reaction or bigger news, snap. Turn: eldest.
F20 School lesson 45s: ch_natalya, pl_school. Topic 0-6 still | explanation 6-18 pan | pupil raises a hand 18-28 push | teacher's life example 28-36 still | turn 36-45 homework or the pupil's line, snap. Adults and the system are funny, never the child. Turn: teacher or pupil.
F21 Doctor 45s: ch_olga, pl_clinic. "На что жалуемся?" 0-5 | complaint 5-15 still | exam 15-25 "дышите", push | diagnosis 25-35 slow push | turn 35-45 the prescription, snap. Turn: doctor.
