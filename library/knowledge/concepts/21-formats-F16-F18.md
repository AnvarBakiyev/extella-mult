# PUPPET format F16-F18: tutorial, support call, startup pitch
tags: format, F16, F17, F18, туториал, поддержка, питч
F16 Tutorial 45s: "Как сделать X за пять шагов". Goal 0-5 still | steps 1-2 5-15 still | steps 3-4 15-30 get strange, push | result 30-38 pull | turn 38-45 step five or an answer to a comment, snap. Turn: presenter.
F17 Support call 45s: ch_sveta + caller, cut between them. Hold music 0-6 "ваш звонок важен" still | greeting 6-10 | problem 10-22 still | script 22-34 "вы пробовали выключить и включить?" push | turn 34-45 the fix, or who is really calling whom, snap. Turn: operator or caller.
F18 Startup pitch 45s: ch_danya, investor ch_viktor, pl_pitch_stage. Problem 0-8 "каждый день миллионы людей..." push | solution 8-18 snap | numbers 18-28 still | investor question 28-36 still | turn 36-45 founder's answer or the jury's decision, snap. Turn: investor.
