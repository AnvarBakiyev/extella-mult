# PUPPET: camera per format
tags: camera, format, map
Interview F01, podcast F15, therapy F07: slow push on guest, near-still host, push on the pause, pull on the false end, snap on the turn.
Talk show F02: pull from host to hall, whip on the surprise guest, snap in silence.
News F03: still anchor, handheld reporter and witness, snap back to the anchor.
Documentary F04, nature F13: pan the landscape, slow push, handheld whisper (F13), pull out at the end.
Ad F05: dutch on the problem, snap on the product, push on the pack shot, push fast on fine print.
Interview job F06, doctor F21, school F20: mostly still, slow push on the answer, snap on the decision.
Court F08: still judge, push on accuser, handheld defence, whip on the witness, shake on the gavel.
Cooking F09, tutorial F16, unboxing F14: pan over the table, handheld process, push on the result.
Weather F10, game F11, press F12, pitch F18: pull on the opener, whip between players, handheld on the hall.
Taxi F22: handheld always. Museum F23, apartment F24: pan rooms, snap on details. Toast F25: handheld, push on the groom.
