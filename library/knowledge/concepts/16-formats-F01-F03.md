# PUPPET format F01-F03: interview, talk show, news
tags: format, F01, F02, F03, интервью, ток-шоу, новости
F01 Interview 45s: host (ch_dina) + guest with dignity, pl_tv_studio. Intro 0-5 host names guest and merit, push | question 5-15 still | deepen 15-30 host grabs a strange detail, guest close-up, push on the pause | false end 30-38 "спасибо, что пришли", pull | turn 38-45 guest one line, snap. Turn: guest.
F02 Talk show 45s: ch_arsen, pl_talkshow. Opening 0-5 host announces topic, pull | story 5-20 guest's drama, slow push | hall 20-25 audience question, handheld | surprise 25-35 "а теперь к нам выходит...", whip | turn 35-45 new guest says the unexpected, snap, silence. Turn: new guest.
F03 News 45s: anchor ch_dina, reporter ch_timur. Anchor 0-6 still | link 6-10 "наш корреспондент на месте" | report 10-25 handheld | witness 25-35 handheld push | turn 35-45 reporter or witness says the real thing, anchor thanks, snap. Turn: witness/reporter.
