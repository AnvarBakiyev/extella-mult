# PUPPET: camera grammar
tags: camera grammar, when to push, snap, dutch, whip, handheld, pull
Serious face, absurd content: the camera behaves like real TV of that genre.
push 0.04-0.06: default for a talking head. Stronger push 0.1 on the strange detail or a confession.
near-still push=0.02: the straight man asking questions; contrast makes the answer funnier.
snap: once or twice per film. Always on the last line or the reaction to it. Not on setup lines.
pull: openings (reveal the studio), the false ending ("спасибо, что пришли"), the final pull-out of a documentary.
handheld: news field reports, reality, unboxing selfie, wedding guests, taxi. Not in a studio interview.
dutch: commercial "problem" beat, lies, guilt, a client in crisis. Never on two shots in a row.
whip: talk-show surprise guest, game-show contestants in turn, family dinner back-and-forth.
pan: landscapes, maps, museum rooms, apartment tours, the table of ingredients.
shake: impacts, gavel, wind at a field report.
Use at least 3 different moves in a film. Never 4 identical shots in a row.
