# PUPPET: commercial break
tags: commercial, ad, реклама, break, ad_cream, jingle
The break line comes from the host, flat, on a strong push or snap: "Извините. Прерываемся на рекламу." It can be the last line of the film.
An ad inside a film: 6-10 s, 1-2 shots, right after the break line.
Product is invented, never a real brand. The product ties back to the episode's absurd premise (salmon milt cream).
Voice: voice "[ad] ..." over the product set (ad_cream). 1-2 lines, 8-14 words total.
Music: music mood=bright tempo=118 over the ad range only. Start it with the ad, stop it when the ad ends.
Text: 1-2 slogans in caps on the empty left: at 0.2,0.25 and 0.24,0.42, color=#c4426a glow=#ffffff, from 0.2 s after the ad starts to its end.
Camera: push=0.06 focus=0.6,0.5 on the product.
After the ad, return to the studio or flip roles (the guest now interviews a person).
F05 film: the whole film is an ad; the turn is the fine print or the slogan.
