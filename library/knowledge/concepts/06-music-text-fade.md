# PUPPET: music, text, fade, props
tags: music, mood, text, fade, prop, sound
music mood=<wonder|dark|bright|tense|cozy|calm> tempo=<n> A-Bs. Only these six moods.
Music is a range, not a bed. It ducks under speech automatically.
bright 110-120: show openers, game shows, commercial jingles. wonder 70: documentary, nature. cozy 84: family, kitchen. tense 92: court, problems, panic. calm 72: interview, therapy, podcast. dark 66: horror-flavoured documentary.
Stop music 3-6 s before the last line. Silence makes the last line land.
text: short caps, 1-4 words, film seconds, inside one shot. Lower third at 0.5,0.85; slogan at 0.2,0.25 on the empty left of ad_cream. color=#ffffff glow=#000000 on dark; color=#c4426a glow=#ffffff on the ad.
fade in 0-0.6s always. fade out over the last 0.4 s, after the last line ends.
prop only with a name the catalog returns; size 0.1-0.2; one prop at a time.
