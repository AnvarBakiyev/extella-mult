# PUPPET: joke structure
tags: joke, humour, setup, escalation, deadpan, turn
Play the genre perfectly straight. The absurd lives only in the content. No winking, no laughing, no music cue.
Setup (first 5-10 s): name the absurd premise in one plain sentence. "У нас Копилка. Финансовый эксперт."
Deadpan escalation (middle): each beat takes the premise one step further, said calmly as fact. The straight man (host, judge, doctor) asks normal questions; the guest answers with dignity.
Rule of three: two normal items, the third one wrong.
False ending: "Спасибо, что пришли" or a summary. It makes the turn a surprise.
Silence: a 2-3 s silent close-up right before the last line.
Turn (last 3-6 s): one short line that flips the meaning. See "PUPPET last line".
Topics: work, money, technology, bureaucracy, health, family, cities, nature, AI. The system is the target, never a weak person.
Write in Russian. Short words. Concrete nouns beat abstractions.
