# PUPPET: dialogue timing
tags: timing, words per second, at, pause, line length
Speech rate: 2 words per second. Live voices are slower, so never go above 2.
Line length = words / 2 seconds + 0.5 s for every [pause] tag. Tags are not words.
Start each line 0.3 s after its shot begins: shot 12-16s -> say ... at 12.3s.
End the shot 0.4 s after the line ends. Shot length = 0.3 + line + 0.4.
Examples: 4 words = 2 s -> shot 2.7 s (use 3). 8 words = 4 s -> shot 4.7 s (use 5). 12 words = 6 s -> 6.7 s.
Keep lines short: 3-8 words. Over 10 words, split into two shots.
One line per shot. Two lines never overlap.
A silent shot (no line) of 2-3 s before the last line is a beat, not a gap; keep it.
Tags at the start of a line set delivery: [deadpan] [tired] [whispering] [sighs] [calm] [pause]. One or two tags, not more.
When check reports real recording lengths, re-time with the same rule and shift every later shot; never cut words.
